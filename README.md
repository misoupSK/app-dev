# One of my Favorite Series: Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint

**Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint (ORV)** is a popular **Webtoon** based on a Korean web novel written by *Sing Shong*.  
It combines action, fantasy, and psychological themes, creating an immersive story about SURVIVAL and FATE.

![Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/omniscient-readers-viewpoint/images/1/1d/Volume_3-newcover.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/177?cb=20230527065605)

---

## let's start with the ✨PLOT✨
The story's main protagonist is **Kim Dokja**(KDJ), an ordinary office worker whose favorite web novel, *Three Ways to Survive the Apocalypse*, suddenly becomes real.  
Since he’s the only one who has read the novel, he becomes the “Omniscient reader”(hence the title lol) who knows what will happen next.  
With the help of his knowledge from the novel and powerful allies like **Yoo Joonghyuk**(YJH) and **Kim Dokja Company**(these are his companions alongside YJH), he fights to change the story’s ending and save humanity.

---

## Let's talk about the Main Characters
These three are the most relevant ones in the story:
- **Kim Dokja** – The protagonist and the only reader of the novel that ended up becoming to reality. His knowledge and wits are the things he uses to navigate the scenarios when they suddenly become reality.
- **Yoo Joonghyuk** – The beloved main character of the original novel who has witnessed countless possibilities due to him having the ability to regress over and over until he finds what he's looking for. (He apparently is described as extremely handsome by various people, including KDJ)
- **Han Sooyoung** – A clever, confident, sarcastic, and highly skilled writer  who joins Kim Dokja and company. She wrote the webnovel *SSSSS-grade Infinite Regressor* which KDJ accused of plagiarizing the other novel, *Three Ways to Survive the Apocalypse*.

---

## Why Do I Like It? Well..
> I love how the story starts out randomly, with the main protagonist being the only unique individual who knows what's going on whilst trying to get the hang of it that his favorite novel is actually happening before his very eyes.
> The bond between the characters and the deep emotional storytelling make it unforgettable. The sensitive topics and psychological themes make it uncomfortable but immersive to read. Especially the personal situations of the main character, **Kim Dokja** which affected his journey along with his allies.
